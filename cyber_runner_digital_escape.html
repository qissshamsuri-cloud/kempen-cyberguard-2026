Race across neon railway tracks, dodge incoming trains, and defeat Cyber Challenges to rescue your run! Lari merentasi landasan siber neon, elak kereta api laju, dan selesaikan Cabaran Siber untuk menyambung larian anda!

HOW TO PLAY / ARAHAN

1
Switch Lanes / Tukar Lorong Swipe Left/Right or press A/D & Arrow keys to switch across 3 railway tracks. Leret Kiri/Kanan atau tekan kekunci A/D & Anak Panah untuk tukar antara 3 landasan.
2

3
Collision Rescue Quiz / Kuiz Penyelamat If you crash, answer a Cybersecurity Challenge correctly to earn +100 pts, a Cyber Shield, and continue running! Jika terlanggar halangan, jawab Cabaran Keselamatan Siber dengan betul untuk +100 mata, Perisai Siber, dan sambung berlari!
